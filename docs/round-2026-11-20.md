## Found

Every branch on the remote is either an ancestor of `main` or a stale
pre-merge snapshot of work `main` already contains; every pull request is
closed or merged. The one commit not in `main` —
`c8de3ff Run the street gate with the Python CI actually installs` — is the
squash of merged PR #7, and `main` carries its content (`scripts/run-streets.mjs`
resolves Python the way CI installs it). **Nothing was left out, and nothing
had to be merged.**

Two changes in their round are worth naming because they tighten the house
rather than loosen it:

* The shared instrument grew a `--require-title` flag and a shared
  `pageReady()` rule that also waits for the loading placeholder to go. The
  project passes the flag, and `scripts/check-spatial-receipt.mjs` still
  refuses any receipt whose cells lack a rendered title. The guarantee from
  the previous round survived the refactor.
* The receipt is now an **assembly**: 41 routes carried over from the sweep of
  15 November, 2 routes re-measured after the cover changed. That is the
  carry-over merge working exactly as it was built to — a reviewer who changes
  one page re-measures that page, and the receipt says plainly that it is an
  assembly and names what it carried.

Their full test suite passes: **299 of 299** (with this round's new test).
It had not been observed finishing on the contended machine; with the machine
idle it runs in 184 seconds.

### 2. The street plates' cue was dead — and the house's own new standard made it obvious

The house has just decided, in the words of its own audit, that an illustration
on a study opening should *lead* the opening: the cover's crayon plate grew
from 127px to 269px on a phone and 440px on a desktop. The five street plates
were left where they were — **64 to 112 pixels wide** — and they are the only
illustration on four of the pages they sit on.

Measured on the live site:

| page | plate | 1440 | 390 |
|---|---|---|---|
| /amend/ | street · Wat Phra Kaew | 112 × 123.9 | 72 × 79.7 |
| /exam/ | street · Ratchadamnoen | 64 × 70.8 | 64 × 70.8 |
| /news/ | street · Sukhumvit | 100 × 110.7 | 72 × 79.7 |
| /read/ | street · Yaowarat | 112 × 123.9 | 72 × 79.7 |
| /topics/ | street · Democracy Monument | 112 × 123.9 | 72 × 79.7 |

The street itself reads at 112px. The **cue** does not: the name was printed
into the picture at 21 points of type in a 760-point picture, so it arrives at
**1.8 to 6.2 points on the screen** — a grey smudge in the corner of a street.
And a name inside a picture is not text: a screen reader had nothing to say
about Ratchadamnoen, and a learner enlarging the text could not read it
either, because the type was already in the pixels and could not grow.

The arithmetic settles it. There is no size at which the house uses these
pictures where a baked name can be read. The name had to become text.

### 3. The number in the cue was a second copy

Each of the five pages already states its own scope in its own text — "16
หมวด · 279 มาตรา" on the exam page, "หมวด 1" and "2/279 มาตรา" on the
reading page, "3 แหล่งข่าว" on the news page. The name of the place is the

### 4. The receipt held a page that had no content of its own

The sweep failed on one cell: `/learn/ @519px — timed out waiting for the
page`. `/learn/` is a forwarder — a client-side `router.replace("/")` and a
link to Home, nothing more. It has no layout of its own to review, and
measuring it is a **race**: the redirect runs as soon as the page runs, and
the gate can arrive on either side of it. The receipt had been holding that
page for weeks, and today the race went the other way.

A receipt of a race is not a receipt. The instrument can now be told that a
page has no content of its own, and it leaves the page alone and records
that it did:

```json
{ "check": "noContentOfItsOwn", "route": "/learn/",
  "why": "a forwarder to / with no content of its own" }
```

### 5. The heartbeat had a hole in it — found by the sweep it was meant to protect

Last round's heartbeat sends a message after every measurement, so that
silence means a dead browser rather than a slow one. It also meant that a
browser stuck on **one** route could keep the sweep alive for ever: the
browser finished width after width of a route that never completed, and every
one of them said hello. The watchdog watched for chatter, and the chatter
never stopped.

So the watchdog now counts the measurement **in flight**, not the browser's
chatter: the harness says which measurement it is starting, the clock starts
there, and the clock restarts for the next measurement. A browser that is
breathing but stuck on one page is caught in three minutes instead of never.

## Changed

### The name of the place is now text, under the picture it belongs to

The tag is gone from the pixels. The picture is a street with a girl in it,
and the page says what the place is called, in the house's own micro type,
the way a plate is captioned in a book.

The name is **data**, written by the script that draws the streets
(`scripts/illustrations/build_streets.py` → `data/street-cues.json`), in
Thai, English and Chinese. The picture and the caption cannot disagree about
which place this is, because one script writes both.

The number the plate turns on stays in that file — it is what the plate cues,
and the release gate checks it against the picture that ships.

### The caption obeys the house's type ramp

The first attempt set the caption at 12px and the layout gate refused it:
*"text is set in 12px, off the house ramp (13 / 18 / 32 / 44)."* A caption is
not a new size; it is the quietest size there is, so it is `var(--micro)` —
13px, and 16px when the reader enlarges the text.

### The receipt is whole again

The 42 real pages were measured together, in one sweep, on the final build —
no carried-over cells, no assembly. The forty-third page is the forwarder,
exempt and named.

### The watchdog counts the measurement, not the chatter

Two changes to the shared instrument, re-vendored here with the sync gate
green: the harness announces the measurement it is **starting**, and the
watchdog counts from that moment; and a page the project declares content-free
is left alone and recorded.
