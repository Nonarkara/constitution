# Understanding-first coaching

Authorized scope: strengthen logical understanding and lasting recall for Tikky and public learners. Existing prediction cases, pastel memory rooms, mixed review and spaced retrieval are retained. Three new layers operate throughout the470-question Quiz: recall before options, optional confidence calibration and teach-back explanations. Timed Exam remains unchanged.

## Evidence and implementation

[Dunlosky et al.2013](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/journals/pspi/learning-techniques.html) rates practice testing and distributed practice highly; explanation/interleaving benefits are more task-dependent. [Carpenter et al.2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-022-00089-1) reviews spacing and retrieval. These support strategy choices, not guaranteed outcomes for this product. Research page labels the schedule heuristic and need for human evaluation. No claim to have applied every psychological technique indiscriminately.

Learners may think, speak or type before revealing choices, or skip the recall layer. Drafts stay only in session memory and are compared with feedback. Confidence is optional, does not modify scoring or scheduling, and produces a first-attempt calibration debrief. Teach-back prompts ask who has authority, which conditions apply, why an alternative fails, and whether the same rule transfers when actors/numbers change. Explicitly saved explanations append to existing notes with a5000-character limit; drafts are not silently saved or machine-graded. No political preference affects assessment.

Session debrief separates first-attempt accuracy from retry correction, identifies concepts to revisit and calculates the earliest existing review date in Bangkok time. Existing records and version4backups retain saved explanations through their existing annotation fields. No account, new remote collection or progress migration.
