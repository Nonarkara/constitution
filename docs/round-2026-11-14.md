# The round the house stopped hanging — Found / Changed / Verified / Remaining

## What was asked

*Continue the work. Some agents have worked on the codebase, so prioritize
change/development orchestration, taking the system to the next shipable
standards.*

Four items were left on the table by the previous round, all of them either
blocked or waiting for the owner:

| Item | Why it was open |
|---|---|
| HTTPS redirect | Wrangler's login is zone-read-only; it needs a token with Zone Settings: Edit |
| Spatial sweep hang | The fix was written against a shared, hash-guarded instrument |
| Header buttons 44 px | Held for the owner's yes, because it touches every page |
| Independent review | The last review was the author's own |

This round took all four, and found that the sweep was not hanging at all.

## Found

### 1. The sweep was not hanging. It was measuring a page that does not exist

`--routes` splits on **commas**. Every sweep script in this project's history
passed **spaces**:

```
node scripts/spatial/spatial-check.mjs --dir out --update --report --routes "/ /along/ /amend/ ..."
```

One argument, one route, and its path is the whole line — a page that is not
in the build. The harness loaded it, waited for it, and timed out; the browser
was restarted with the same impossible instruction; the watchdog saw six
minutes of silence and called it a hang. **That is what the hang was.** The
machine's contention was real, but it was not the cause.

The cost was not a slow sweep. It was a sweep that could never finish and a
failure that looked like a broken browser.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| what the gate was asked to measure | one page named `/ /along/ /amend/ …` | 43 real pages |
| attempts before it gave up | 12, each after 6 minutes of silence | none — one sweep, no stall |
| result | 72 minutes, zero measurements | 43 routes, 2,709 cells, no drift |

The instrument now also **fails fast and names the route** it does not have,
before a browser is started:

```
FAIL — 1 of the 1 route(s) asked for are not in this build.
  ✗ /reed/
  Nothing was measured. Run the gate without --routes to see the pages this build has.
  (Routes are separated by COMMAS: --routes "/,/read/".)
```

estimated, at the three widths the house law names:

| width | header | ไทย | EN | 中文 | themes (min height) |

| 1440 | 53 px | 48 × 44 | 45.9 × 44 | 54 × 44 | 44 |
| 1024 | 53 px | 48 × 44 | 45.9 × 44 | 54 × 44 | 44 |

The owner's own commit (`fdf4d81a`, 3 November) had already applied

was zero, because the change was already made.

### 3. The site answered plain HTTP with the whole constitution in clear

`http://constitution.nonarkara.org/` returned **200** and the full study
application. HSTS was in the headers, so a browser that had seen it once
would never ask again — but a link, a scan, a first visit, or a curl got the
page unencrypted.

The zone's own "Always Use HTTPS" toggle would fix this. It cannot be reached
from here: the project's token carries `zone:read` and nothing more, and the
zone settings endpoint answers `9109 Unauthorized to access requested
resource`; the rulesets endpoint answers `10000 Authentication error`. The
Cloudflare API is closed to this project.

But the Worker can see the plain-HTTP request — it already answered `308` for
`/api/news` — and the Worker was simply not allowed to see the pages.

### 4. The avenue plate was a brown desk pasted on a road

The five street plates render at **100 × 110.7 px** on the desktop and
**72 × 79.7 px** on a phone, from a source of 760 × 841 — thirteen per cent.
The cue printed inside each picture is 21 px of type in a 760 px picture:
**2.8 px on the screen**. The credit burned into the bottom of each is about
1.3 px. Neither can be read at the size the house actually uses.

At that size the five plates were looked at side by side, and four read.
**Ratchadamnoen did not:** its figure was 44% of the picture's height — a
girl at a school desk, pasted over the corner of the road, with the pastel's
soft white aura around it. A quarter of the picture was furniture.

## Changed

### The site is HTTPS-only, everywhere

The Worker now answers any plain-HTTP request with a redirect to HTTPS,
keeping the path and the query string, before the asset is served. The
Worker runs first for **every** request, not only for `/api/*` — that is what
lets it reach the pages at all.

```js
if (url.protocol === "http:")
  return Response.redirect(`https://${url.host}${url.pathname}${url.search}`, 308);
```

Four tests prove it before it can reach the public domain: a plain-HTTP
request for any page redirects, the path and query survive, an HTTPS request
is served rather than redirected, and the API keeps its own methods, headers
and cache. The suite is 295 tests, all green.

### The instrument retires its browser on purpose, and reports every measurement

Four changes to the **canonical** instrument (re-vendored here; the sync gate
is green):

1. **The browser is retired every twelve routes** and a fresh one takes the
   rest. It used to be given work after it had already begun to wedge.
2. **The harness sends a heartbeat after every single measurement.** It used
   to speak once per route — sixty-three measurements deep — so silence was
   indistinguishable from slowness.
3. **The stall threshold fell from six minutes to three.** With a heartbeat
   per measurement, three minutes of silence is a dead browser: the slowest a
   single measurement can be is bounded by the harness itself (30 s to
   arrive, 12 s to render, 5 s to settle).
4. **An unknown route is named, immediately**, before a browser is started.

### The avenue plate was put back in the street

The figure is now at **the street's own scale** — she is the size of the
pedestrian the drawing already contains, standing where that pedestrian
stands, and the street keeps its own people.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| figure height | 44% of the picture | 19% |
| where she stands | on the near road | on the far pavement, beside the street's own pedestrian |
| the pastel's white aura | around the paste | trimmed off the drawing's edge |

The aura is trimmed by eroding the alpha channel before the drawing is laid
down. A crayon edge belongs to a crayon; on a line drawing in screentone a
white rim is what the eye calls a sticker.

The other four plates were looked at the same way, at full size and at 100 px.
They read. Sukhumvit was checked most closely, because its figure also sits on
a stool: it is at the edge of the lane, the stool is small, and there is no
blob. **Ratchadamnoen was the outlier and it is fixed.**

### The street builder now records its own bytes

The five scenes keep their own ground, so the illustration manifest records
their bytes rather than re-deriving them. That record has to be written by the
thing that makes the scenes, or the two drift — and the gate then reports that
the file on disk is not the file that was recorded, which is true and useless.
`build_streets.py` now writes both manifests in one command.

### The receipt says which build it is a receipt of

The receipt recorded the measurements and never the tree they were taken from.
The build binding existed — the previous agent had written it by hand — but
the instrument did not, and a binding written by hand is a binding that stops
being written the first time nobody remembers. The instrument now fingerprints
the directory it measured (2,046 files, hashed path-and-bytes in order) and
writes it into the receipt.

### The receipt no longer deletes the pages it did not measure

This one was found by doing it. Recording six routes at a time, on a machine
that reboots between chunks, **wrote a receipt of six routes over a receipt
of forty-three.** The instrument's own comment promised that an assembly
would carry the unmeasured routes over; the code did not do it, and
`carriedOver` was a hardcoded `[]`.

Now it does what it says:

```
routes in the copy: 43
measuredTogether: false
carriedOver: 42 routes
measured: 63 | carried cells: 2646
```

A receipt that is an assembly says so, and names what it carried.

### The export carries no Finder metadata

The release gate failed on `.DS_Store` in the export — macOS writes one into
any folder a person opens in Finder, and the export copies `public/`
wholesale. The gate caught it, which is why it was caught; but a gate that
catches it *after* the build means every build on a machine with a Finder
open is a build that has to be rebuilt somewhere else. The export is now
cleaned where the export is made.

### The release check is a command, not a one-off

`scripts/verify-live.mjs` compares the public domain against the build: every
page body, every asset the offline manifest lists, the site's own answer to
plain HTTP, and the Thai text that the whole re-spacing was for. It writes the
evidence record. The previous release check was a session's worth of ad-hoc
probes. It retries, because a machine under load runs out of ephemeral ports
and a release check that dies of a socket error reports nothing about the
release.

## Verified

* **The live site and the build are the same bytes.** 43 of 43 page bodies
  match byte for byte, and 361 of 361 assets the offline manifest lists match
  byte for byte. The live site's own revision is `034e2316b9b7f4c4`; the
  build's is the same.
* **The live site carries the re-spaced Thai**, and no pre-re-spacing text:
  `หน่วยงานปฏิเสธบริการเพียง เพราะผู้ขอ เป็นผู้หญิง` is present, the
  unbroken string is not.
* **Plain HTTP now redirects**, with the path and the query string kept:
  `http://constitution.nonarkara.org/read/?section=99` →
  `https://constitution.nonarkara.org/read/?section=99` (308).
* **Nothing else broke in the deploy.** Eleven routes answer 200, `/api/news`
  answers 200, a missing page answers 404 with the house's own trilingual
  page, the edge cache still reports `CF-Cache-Status: HIT` for assets served
  through the Worker, and HSTS, CSP and `X-Content-Type-Options` are all
  still on the response.
* **The sweep passes on every route** — 43 routes × 63 widths = 2,709
  measurements, measured together, with no grown, shrunk, gone or unexplained
  finding. It finished in **one attempt with no stall at all**; the browser was
  **retired three times** during the run, on purpose, at routes 12, 24 and 36.
  The sweep that preceded it, on the same build, stalled nine times.
* **The receipt names its build**: 2,046 files, fingerprint
  `80d8c0465dbba9ce38a8594e2e0c0081c67d2b0ced50d871b8b055505a4ff874`.
* **The illustration gate passes**: 35 plates measured for real transparency,
  10 re-derived byte for byte from their masters, no interior holes, and 10
  scenes keeping their own ground.
* **295 of 295 tests pass**, including the four new HTTPS tests.
* **The other agents' work is already here.** Every branch on the remote is
  either an ancestor of `main` or a stale pre-merge snapshot of work `main`
  already contains; every pull request is closed or merged. Nothing was left
  out, and nothing had to be merged.

## Remaining

* **The cue inside the street plates is still unreadable at the size they are
  rendered.** The tag that names the place is 2.8 px on screen; the credit
  burned into the bottom of each picture is about 1.3 px. The credit is
  carried properly by the Sources page and by `data/sources/streets.json`,
  but the copy that says *"this credit is printed into the bottom of every
  picture"* is true of the file and not of the rendered picture. The fix is
  either a caption in the DOM, where the house's micro type is legible at the
  same footprint, or a bigger plate. Both change the layout of five pages and
  want the owner's eye before they are taken.
* **Real-device Safari, touch gestures and a full keyboard walkthrough** were
  outside the browser audit, as before.
* **The other four byte-identical projects** that vendor the shared
  instrument were deliberately not propagated: the canonical copy changed, and
  copying new bytes into those projects without re-baselining their receipts
  would break four gates for a change that is not theirs yet.
