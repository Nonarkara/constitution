# Pastel constitutional memory atlas

Built-in imagegen, 12 October 2026. Five original generated compositions; source PNGs preserved. User directed pastel crayons, small charming figures, meaningful visual jokes and number memory cues. No artwork is claimed to be hand-drawn. No real portrait or party caricature. Numbers are rendered as text by the app for accuracy rather than invented inside drawings.

Shared prompt: Four separate equal-width scenes in one horizontal atlas strip, clean white ground, roomy gutters. Recurring adult Thai woman with shoulder-length dark-brown hair and pale butter-yellow sweater. Soft pastel dusty blush, sage, powder blue, cream and faded lavender. Light scratchy wax crayon/colored pencil, imperfect brown outlines, diary sketches and gentle visual jokes. Avoid saturated colors, vector polish, heavy outlines, words, numerals, logos and official insignia.

- Atlas1: indivisible puzzle and tugging duck; constitutional book under ceremonial umbrella, sparrow with tiny umbrella; peaceful public square, duck with huge blank speech bubble; rubbish sorting and plant care, duck with oversized helmet.
- Atlas2: preschool desk and book stairs, turtle with oversized schoolbag; law-draft consultation table, duck with giant chair; two chamber tables, duck struggling to fit chair through door; cabinet tea table, cat in one large kettle and many small cups.
- Atlas3: private wallet pulled by magnet near public desk, duck stuck in slippers; independent court scale, cat tries adding biscuit under blocking umbrella; nine chairs, hourglass, clock and cat with giant gavel; five birdwatcher booths, one bird uses binoculars backwards.
- Atlas4: state attorney’s legal folders, duck struggles with huge book; local houses/municipal desk/garden, cat directing bikes; amendment doors, real keys, duck tries banana and two hourglasses; seven tool drawers and seedlings, duck with too-large hard hat fixing little bridge.
- Civic panorama edit: preserve door/ballot/square/budget four stations of the earlier original, soften to pastels. Add duck trying banana key, cat at ballot box with upside-down blank ballot, sparrow with giant blank speech bubble and duck balancing tiny coin/large carrot. Keep human civic activities clear.

Each atlas is2172×724; the app clips four equal panels using CSS, preserving the source without raster alterations. Served as1600px WebP. Atlas pictures are mnemonic scenes, not literal depictions of every institution, counted object or procedure. Captions and section links supply legal meaning.

Three companion edits use the same pastel pencil/crayon direction, preserving original poses/learning actions: reading gains a duck with oversized glasses; listening gains one earmuff on the sleeping cat; review gains a duck holding a blank card upside-down. Original PNGs/WebPs retained; new siblings use the -pastel suffix. Served at720px. Built-in imagegen edits only, no CLI model substitution.
